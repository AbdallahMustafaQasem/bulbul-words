Privacy Policy - Bulbul Words

Effective date: 2026-10-21
App: Bulbul Words (Arabic name: بلبل الكلمات), package com.amq.bulbulwords
Developer contact: abdallahmqasem@gmail.com

Summary

Bulbul Words is a vocabulary and speech-practice app for young children. It has no accounts, no in-app purchases and no analytics. Words, games and progress work fully offline. The app shows ads from Google AdMob that are configured for children (see "Ads" below). The app itself does not collect or share personal information.

What we do not collect

  • The app itself does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise locations, photos or contacts.
  • We do not record audio. The app does not use the microphone.
  • We do not use the Android advertising ID. The app removes the advertising-ID permission. We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of our own.
  • We have no servers and send no data to our own servers. The app uses the internet only so that Google AdMob can load ads.
  • We do not sell or rent data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, described below.

What is stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves a small amount of information locally on your device only, using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Your settings (language, speech speed, sound options, calm mode).
  • Optionally, the child's first name, if a parent types it in the parent area. It is used only to greet the child inside the app.
  • Learning progress: stars, daily goal and which words or games were practiced.

This information never leaves the device. We cannot see it and have no access to it.

Text-to-speech

The app speaks words using the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (for example Google Text-to-Speech). The words to be spoken are passed to that engine on your device. The engine is provided by your device or its manufacturer and is governed by its own privacy policy. Bulbul Words does not control or receive any data from it.

Children's privacy

  • The app is designed for children, including children under 13, and is intended to be used with parental involvement.
  • In line with COPPA and the Google Play Families Policy, the app does not collect personal information from children, shows only ads served under Google's child-directed treatment (no personalised ads, no advertising ID, maximum content rating G) from Google Play certified ad networks, does not offer purchases, and does not let children communicate with others or reach external websites (apart from the ads described below, which are shown outside the child areas).
  • The parent area is protected by a password that the parent chooses on first use. The password is kept only on the device as a salted SHA-256 hash, is never sent anywhere and cannot be recovered. If it is forgotten, clearing the app's data from the device settings resets it and also deletes progress.
  • Entering a child's first name is optional. We recommend using a first name or nickname only.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads. Because the app is designed for children:

  • Every ad request is marked as child-directed and as coming from users under the age of consent, so ads are not personalised and no interest-based advertising is used.
  • Only ads rated for general audiences (G) are requested, from Google Play certified ad networks.
  • The advertising ID is not used.
  • Ads are never shown during a game or while a child is learning words. They appear only in the password-protected parent area, after a finished game at most once every few minutes, when a parent unlocks the app after the play-time limit, and when the app is reopened after a long break.
  • To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may process limited technical data such as the device IP address, device and operating-system information and basic app-interaction and diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under its own policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

The app contains no other third-party advertising, analytics, social, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

Parents' rights and data deletion

Because all data stays on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • To change or remove the child's first name, open the parent area and edit the name.
  • To delete all data, go to Android Settings > Apps > Bulbul Words > Storage > Clear data (or Clear storage). Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data.
  • Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete on our side. If you have questions, contact us at the email below.

Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox. The app does not send the information it stores to any server; the only network traffic is the loading of ads by Google AdMob.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app changes. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date at the top will be updated. If a change affects how children's information is handled, we will update the app's store listing and this policy before the change takes effect.
