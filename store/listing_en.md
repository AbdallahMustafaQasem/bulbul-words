## Full description (max 4000)

Bulbul Words is a calm, colorful, offline app that helps young children learn everyday words and practice saying them out loud. It is designed for ages 2-5 and for children who need extra gentle, repetitive practice, such as children with speech delay or autism.

Every word is shown as a clear picture and spoken aloud, so a child can see, hear and then try to say it.

WORDS YOUR CHILD MEETS EVERY DAY
- 420+ words in 15 categories: fruits, vegetables, food, clothes, animals, birds, body parts, home, transport, family, places, actions, feelings, numbers and letters
- Some words use hand-drawn pictures; the rest use simple emoji
- Letters and numbers follow the chosen language: Arabic or English
- Words are spoken by your device's text-to-speech voice

CHOOSE HOW THE WORDS ARE SPOKEN
- Modern Standard Arabic
- English
- Adjustable speech speed, so words can be played slowly and clearly

12 SHORT, SIMPLE GAMES
- Choose the picture
- Memory
- Odd one out
- Shapes
- Puzzle
- Colors
- Animal sounds
- Balloons
- Counting
- Say the word
- Sorting
- Shadow match

Each game is short and repeatable, with positive feedback and stars for effort.

GENTLE MOTIVATION
- Stars after each round
- A daily goal to build a small routine
- A progress screen so you can see which words your child practiced
- The app can greet your child by first name (optional, kept on the device only)

A CALM EXPERIENCE
- Calm mode for children who are sensitive to sound and motion
- Sound toggles to switch effects and music on or off
- No time pressure and no punishing "game over" screens

FOR PARENTS
- The parent area is locked with a password that parents choose the first time they open it, so little hands cannot change settings
- Settings: language, child's first name, speech speed, sound options, calm mode

PRIVACY AND SAFETY
- No ads
- No in-app purchases
- No accounts or sign-in
- No internet access needed and no internet permission
- No analytics, no tracking, no data sent anywhere
- The child's first name and progress stay only on your device

A NOTE ON SPEECH AND LEARNING
Bulbul Words is an educational practice tool. It supports learning at home and can complement the work of speech-language therapists and teachers, but it does not diagnose, treat or replace professional therapy or advice. If you have concerns about your child's speech or development, please speak with a qualified specialist.

REQUIREMENTS
The app uses your device's text-to-speech engine (for example Google Text-to-Speech). If a voice for your chosen language is missing, install it from your device's text-to-speech settings. Voice quality depends on the voice installed on your device.
