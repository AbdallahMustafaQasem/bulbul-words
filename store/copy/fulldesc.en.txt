Bulbul Words is a calm, colorful app that helps young children learn everyday words and practice saying them out loud. It is made for ages 2 to 5, and for any child who needs gentle, repeated practice, such as children with a speech delay.

Every word appears as a clear picture and is spoken aloud, so a child can see the word, hear it and then try to say it. The whole app works offline.

WORDS YOUR CHILD MEETS EVERY DAY
- 420+ words in 15 categories: fruits, vegetables, food, clothes, animals, birds, body parts, home, transport, family, places, actions, feelings, numbers and letters
- Letters and numbers follow the language you choose: Arabic or English
- Adjustable speech speed, so words can be played slowly and clearly

20 SHORT, SIMPLE GAMES
- Choose the picture
- Memory
- Odd one out
- Shapes and colors
- Puzzle
- Animal sounds
- Balloons and counting
- Say the word
- Sorting and shadow match
- And many more

Each game is short and can be repeated, with gentle encouragement for every try.

CALM MOTIVATION
- Stars after each round
- A daily goal to build a small routine
- A sticker album that fills as your child learns new words
- A progress screen showing which words your child practiced
- No time pressure and no harsh game over screens

FOR PARENTS
- The parent area is locked with a password that you choose yourself, so little hands cannot change settings
- Set a play-time limit, and a gentle rest screen appears when time is up
- Short tips for parents on practicing with your child at home
- Settings for language, speech speed and sounds

PRIVACY
- No ads in this version
- No accounts or sign-in
- Works offline
- No data is sent anywhere, and your child's progress stays on your device

AN IMPORTANT NOTE
Bulbul Words is an educational practice tool for use at home. It supports your child's learning and can complement the work of speech therapists and teachers, but it does not diagnose, treat or replace speech therapy or professional advice. If you are concerned about your child's speech or development, please talk to a qualified specialist.

REQUIREMENTS
The app speaks words using a voice installed on your device. To hear Arabic words, an Arabic voice must be installed in your device's text-to-speech settings. Speech quality depends on the installed voice.
