Privacy Policy - Bulbul Games

Effective date: 2026-10-21
App: Bulbul Games (Arabic name: ألعاب بلبل), package com.amq.bulbulgames
Developer contact: abdallahmqasem@gmail.com

Summary

Bulbul Games is a collection of short educational games (numbers, letters, logic and fun) for children. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no in-app purchases and no chat. The games work offline. The app shows ads from Google AdMob that are configured for children, and uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous game events (see below). The app does not ask for any personal information.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise locations, photos, contacts or messages.
  • We do not record audio. The app does not use the microphone or the camera.
  • We do not use the Android advertising ID. The app removes the advertising-ID permission, and ad personalisation and ad storage are switched off for analytics.
  • We have no accounts and no servers of our own. We do not sell or rent data.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally, using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Settings (language, sound on or off).
  • Game progress: the number of stars earned in each game.

This information stays on the device. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Text-to-speech

Some games read numbers and words aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (for example Google Text-to-Speech). The engine is provided by your device or its manufacturer and is governed by its own privacy policy. Bulbul Games does not receive any data from it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). Because the app is designed for children:

  • Every ad request is marked as child-directed and as coming from users under the age of consent, so ads are not personalised and no interest-based advertising is used.
  • Only ads rated for general audiences (G) are requested, from Google Play certified ad networks.
  • The advertising ID is not used.
  • Ads are never shown while a game is being played. They appear on menu screens (banner), after a finished game at most once every few minutes (full-screen), when the app is reopened after a long break, and when a child chooses to watch an ad to get a hint.
  • To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may process limited technical data such as the device IP address, device and operating-system information and basic app-interaction and diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under its own policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the games are used so that we can improve them. It records anonymous events such as which game was started or completed, the number of stars earned and the chosen language, together with basic technical information (app version, device model, operating system, approximate country derived from the IP address, and a random app-instance identifier). It does not record names or any information that identifies a child. Analytics is configured without the advertising ID, without ad personalisation and without ad storage. More information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Children's privacy

  • The app is designed for children, including children under 13, and in line with COPPA and the Google Play Families Policy it does not ask for or collect personal information from children, does not allow children to communicate with others, and does not offer purchases.
  • Ads are served only under Google's child-directed treatment as described above.
  • Tapping an ad can open an external website or the Google Play store, as ads are provided by Google's certified networks. Parents can prevent this by supervising use, or by turning off the device's internet connection while the child plays; the games work offline.

Parents' rights and data deletion

  • To delete the data stored on the device, go to Android Settings > Apps > Bulbul Games > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • The app holds no account or profile that identifies a child. If you have questions about analytics or ads data, contact us at the email below.
  • You can reset the advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Google settings; this app does not use the advertising ID.

Security

Data on the device is kept in the app's private storage and protected by Android's app sandbox. Data sent to Google AdMob and Firebase is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app changes. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date at the top will be updated. If a change affects how children's information is handled, we will update the app's store listing and this policy before the change takes effect.
